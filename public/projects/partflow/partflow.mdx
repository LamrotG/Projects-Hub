# Overview

PartFlow is a cloud-based spare parts management system designed to replace spreadsheet-driven inventory tracking with a centralized business platform.

The application combines inventory management, sales processing, customer records, supplier management, employee administration, and business reporting into a unified system. It was designed with a focus on maintainability, usability, and providing a complete workflow for managing day-to-day operations within a spare parts business.

---

# Background

Small and medium-sized businesses frequently rely on spreadsheets or disconnected tools to manage inventory, sales, suppliers, and customer information.

While these approaches are familiar, they often lead to duplicated data, inconsistent stock levels, limited reporting capabilities, and increased manual effort.

PartFlow addresses these challenges by providing a single source of truth for business operations, enabling users to manage inventory and transactions through an integrated web application.

---

# Problem

Managing spare parts inventory involves multiple interconnected processes.

Businesses need to:

* track product quantities
* process sales accurately
* maintain supplier relationships
* manage customer information
* monitor employee activity
* analyze business performance

Without a centralized system, maintaining consistency across these processes becomes increasingly difficult as operations grow.

---

# Objectives

The project was built with the following goals:

* Centralize inventory and business operations.
* Reduce manual stock management.
* Automate inventory updates after sales.
* Secure access through authentication and role-based permissions.
* Provide business insights through dashboards and reporting.
* Create a scalable architecture suitable for future expansion.

---

# Solution

PartFlow provides an integrated management platform where all operational data is connected.

The application enables users to authenticate securely, manage products, process sales, maintain supplier and customer records, and monitor business performance through a centralized dashboard.

Each module contributes to a unified workflow, ensuring that inventory changes, business records, and reporting remain synchronized across the application.

---

# Key Features

## Authentication & Authorization

The platform uses Supabase Authentication to secure access.

Features include:

* secure login
* authenticated sessions
* role-based permissions
* administrator and employee accounts

Access to application features is determined by user roles, helping protect operational data.

---

## Product & Inventory Management

Products and inventory are managed through dedicated interfaces that allow users to:

* create products
* update inventory
* monitor stock levels
* maintain product records

Inventory serves as the operational foundation for sales and reporting.

---

## Sales Management

Sales transactions automatically update inventory, reducing manual stock adjustments and helping maintain accurate inventory records.

The workflow connects customer information, product inventory, and sales history into a consistent transactional process.

---

## Customer Management

Customer records are stored centrally, allowing sales activities to be associated with customer information while improving data organization.

---

## Supplier Management

Supplier records support inventory procurement workflows and maintain a structured list of business partners.

---

## Employee Management

Administrative users can manage employee accounts and control access through predefined roles.

This enables multiple staff members to collaborate while maintaining operational security.

---

## Dashboard & Analytics

A centralized dashboard presents key business metrics through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard provides quick insights into operational performance and supports informed decision-making.

---

## Audit Logging

User activities are recorded to improve traceability and accountability throughout the system.

Audit logs provide visibility into operational changes while supporting administrative oversight.

---

# Architecture

The application follows a modular full-stack architecture using the Next.js App Router.

```text
Presentation Layer

↓

Authentication

Inventory

Sales

Customers

Suppliers

Employees

Analytics

↓

Business Logic

↓

Supabase Services

↓

PostgreSQL Database
```

Each business module is responsible for a specific domain while sharing common authentication, data access, and UI components.

This separation promotes maintainability and future scalability.

* reduce unnecessary interactions

---

# Engineering Challenges

Developing PartFlow required addressing several interconnected challenges:

* synchronizing inventory after sales
* maintaining relationships between multiple business entities
* implementing secure role-based access
* designing reusable CRUD interfaces
* presenting analytics in an intuitive format
* keeping the application modular as features expanded

These challenges reinforced the importance of separating business logic from presentation and designing reusable components.

---

# Lessons Learned

Building PartFlow provided valuable experience in designing business applications.

Key takeaways include:

* Business domains benefit from clear separation of responsibilities.
* Authentication should be integrated early in application development.
* Relational databases naturally model interconnected business data.
* Reusable UI components improve long-term maintainability.
* Consistent data validation reduces operational errors.
* Dashboards should prioritize actionable information over excessive metrics.
